## Style Prompt
A modern, elegant, and dynamic promotional video for "ReisOrient", a real-time tracking app for the Three Kings parade. It should feel premium, reliable, and magical.

## Colors
- Background: #0f172a (Slate 900)
- Primary Accent: #fbbf24 (Amber 400 - Gold for the Kings)
- Secondary Accent: #8b5cf6 (Violet 500 - Royal Purple)
- Text Primary: #f8fafc (Slate 50)
- Text Secondary: #cbd5e1 (Slate 300)

## Typography
- Primary: 'Inter', sans-serif

## What NOT to Do
- No generic bright red/green.
- No slow, boring fades. Keep motion snappy and fluid.
- No cluttered screens.
